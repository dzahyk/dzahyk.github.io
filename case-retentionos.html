WHERE IT STARTED

A retention spec that could not survive its own numbers.

RetentionOS started as my own spec for business broadband. Before building it, I reviewed the spec: 39 gaps, 11 critical. No churn label, churn probability stored as an input, and an ROI formula that rewarded discounting everyone.

From the gap analysis

The mockup’s “net saved Rp840 million” for an account paying Rp45 million a month is impossible; the upper bound is about Rp396 million.

WHAT I CHANGED

Four fixes to my own spec.

Each closes a gap from the review.

  1. 01

    Protected value, not full CLV

    Offer value = drop in churn probability × customer value. Stayers get the discount too, so blind discounting loses money.

  2. 02

    Evaluation in the open

    Holdout by account and time, grouped cross-validation, confidence intervals, a naive baseline and calibration — not one accuracy number.

  3. 03

    Four-eyes approval

    Large or unrecommended offers wait for a retention manager. Nobody approves their own offer, and a rejection needs a reason.

  4. 04

    Nothing goes to the customer

    It only drafts messages. The spec’s “apply offer & email” button had no control, so I removed it.

THE APP

Four views around one call queue.

A risk radar, an account panel, cohort survival with RFM migration, an accuracy page, and an approval log.

Who works from the queue
UserMain question
Account managerWhich of my accounts should I call first, and what should I offer?
Retention managerIs this incentive worth its cost, and should I approve it?

The guide page opens on the product name set behind a cut-out miniature town, the tallest roofs covering its base, over the line “Know earlier which customers may leave, and what to do about it.” As you scroll, the town rises over the name; all motion switches off when the device asks for reduced motion.

TESTS & LIMITS

85 tests, and only 16 churn events to test against.

  • Not yet: an A/B test of real offers, or accuracy on real customer data.
  • Confidence intervals are wide. There is no authentication: the role picker is a demo mode.
What the 85 tests cover

Data and model, analytics and ROI, API contracts, security hardening, approval rules, and the facts in drafted messages: “no open tickets” is never written when ticket data is missing.

Accounts are generated with a fixed seed; no real customer or operator data is used.
