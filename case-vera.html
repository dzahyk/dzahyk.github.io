THREE DOCUMENTS

Three documents, arriving on different days.

The order, the goods, and the bill rarely arrive together. Finance has to tell what was ordered, what actually arrived, and what is being charged — and decide before paying, with the evidence in view.

From the product spec

Connect the purchase order, the physical receipt, and the invoice; show every mismatch with its evidence; prepare a decision that can be traced.

Each document matters to a different person:

UserMain question
Finance managerCan I approve this invoice, and what exactly am I approving?
AP adminWhich invoices need a revision or a check?
Warehouse staffWhat arrived today, and in what condition?
RULES I KEPT

A status never means more than it does.

Four places where the easy version would have overstated what happened.

  1. 01

    A correction to the spec

    The spec’s example gave Rp42,500,000 after a 50-unit return. 1,000 units at Rp45,000 minus 50 returned is Rp42,750,000, so the app uses that and the difference is documented.

  2. 02

    Ready to pay is not paid

    Approval moves an invoice to Ready to pay. The CSV export is labelled internal review and never marks anything as paid.

  3. 03

    Roles decide actions

    Managers approve, AP admins draft, and warehouse staff never see prices. Duplicate invoices and missing signatures stay blocked for everyone.

  4. 04

    Offline that tells the truth

    Warehouse photos are stored on the device and labelled “saved on device”, never “sent”, until a server acknowledges them.

THE FRONTEND

Five menus and one case workspace.

Five lifecycle menus, a case workspace with an origin trail and evidence, printable debit notes, an internal-review CSV, purchase-order import, and a mobile warehouse flow with camera capture. The landing page animates the check itself: a scan line crosses the invoice table, the mismatched row is flagged, then the approval seal stamps — and the loop pauses when it is off screen.

LIMITS

A frontend, not a payment system.

Main flows were checked in the browser and with Playwright: approval with a checklist and reason, CSV download, persistence after reload, an offline reload, and rejection of an invalid CSV without a partial import.

  • Not yet: OCR, a backend with server-side roles, or ERP, email, and bank connections.
  • The role picker is a demo, not security. Data lives in this browser and can be cleared by the browser.
